About Our Research

Welcome to the Metagenomics & Translatomics Research Group. Our team focuses on deciphering the complex microbial communities in various ecological niches using cutting-edge sequencing technologies and bioinformatics approaches.

We integrate metagenomic, metatranscriptomic, and clinical data to uncover novel insights into host-microbe interactions, disease mechanisms, and potential therapeutic targets. Our resources include curated datasets, genome catalogues, and open-access publications.

Explore our database to browse samples, culture collections, taxonomy assignments, and download analytical datasets.
